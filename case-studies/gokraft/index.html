[01] The Challenge

Breaking dependence on referrals.

GoKraft is a premier event management and AV solutions provider. Their work is excellent — stage setups, lighting rigs, corporate AV execution that speaks for itself. But excellent work and a predictable pipeline are not the same thing.

Contract flow was driven by word-of-mouth and seasonal influxes. When referrals slowed, so did revenue. To grow aggressively into enterprise corporate accounts without spending 6–8 months building an in-house SDR team, they needed a direct, repeatable line to decision-makers.

The constraint
"Scale aggressively into enterprise accounts — without spending 6–8 months building an in-house SDR team."
What they needed
A direct line to CHROs, HR Heads, and Marketing Leads in companies with 100+ employees — verified, targeted, and running before the next corporate event season.

[02] The Approach

Synchronized LinkedIn + Email.

Instead of a single channel, we built an integrated dual-track acquisition system — LinkedIn for trust and visibility, email for direct reach. Both tracks hit the same accounts simultaneously.

01 / Targeting

Precision ICP Targeting

We curated verified lists of the exact decision-makers who control corporate event and AV budgets — not broad marketing databases.

  • CHROs, HR Heads, and Marketing Leads only
  • Enterprise companies with 100+ employees
  • Contact data verified via Apollo.io
  • Lists aligned to upcoming event calendar windows
02 / LinkedIn

LinkedIn Connection Engine

LinkedIn built the trust layer — using GoKraft's own stage setups and AV execution as social proof to warm decision-makers before email arrived.

  • Targeted connection campaigns via Sales Navigator
  • Visual proofs of GoKraft's corporate AV work
  • Rapport-building conversations before any pitch
  • Multi-touch message sequences post-connect
03 / Email

6-Touch Email Sequences

Distinct 6-touch email campaigns with angle shifts at each touch — timed to corporate event calendar cycles, not generic cadences.

  • Multi-inbox campaigns via Instantly
  • Dedicated sending domains on Google Workspace
  • Deliverability monitoring across all domains
  • Sequences built around event season timing
