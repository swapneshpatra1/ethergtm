[01] The Challenge

More than sending emails.

Effective B2B email outreach takes more than hitting send. It needs properly configured sending infrastructure, relevant prospect data, personalized messaging, and a controlled approach to deliverability.

Ashtakona needed a structured outbound email system that brought all of these together — without its team having to manage multiple technical tools and workflows.

The constraint
"Launch B2B email outreach without our team managing multiple technical tools and workflows."
What they needed
Sending infrastructure, prospect data, personalized messaging, and deliverability control — set up and run as one managed system.

[02] The Solution

Managed end to end by EtherGTM.

We handled the email infrastructure and the outbound workflow — from technical configuration to prospect research and campaign preparation.

01 / Infrastructure

Email Infrastructure & Authentication

The technical foundation was configured and checked before a single campaign email went out.

  • Agreed sending domains and mailboxes configured
  • SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records set up where applicable
  • Sender accounts connected to the outreach platform
  • Authentication and sending setup reviewed pre-launch
02 / Prospecting

B2B Prospect Database

Apollo was used to find relevant B2B companies and the decision-makers inside them.

  • Companies and contacts sourced from Apollo
  • Selected on company and contact information
  • Database aligned to Ashtakona's audience
03 / Warm-up & Sending

Warm-Up & Campaign Sending

Instantly managed mailbox warm-up and sending — building consistent sending behavior instead of scaling volume too early.

  • Mailbox warm-up before campaign launch
  • Controlled sending volumes
  • Follow-up sequences
  • Ongoing campaign monitoring
04 / Personalization

Account-Based Personalization

Claude turned available account-level information into more relevant messaging, instead of relying on generic templates.

  • Emails tailored to each company
  • Grounded in the account's business context
  • Focused on likely areas of relevance
