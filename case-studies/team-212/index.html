[01] The Challenge

Reaching leaders with no spare time.

Founders and senior executives spend their days managing businesses, making decisions, and balancing competing priorities. Reaching them takes an approach that feels relevant to both their professional and personal goals.

Team 212 is a personal development and accountability community that helps people create greater harmony across their lives through time management, energy, accountability, and mindset.

The challenge was to connect Team 212 with US-based founders, C-suite executives, and senior leaders who might resonate with its philosophy — and to build a consistent flow of relevant conversations through LinkedIn.

The constraint
"Reach busy founders and executives in a way that feels relevant — not like another sales pitch."
What they needed
A consistent flow of LinkedIn conversations with US founders, C-suite executives, and senior leaders who share Team 212's view of performance and well-being.

[02] The Approach

A focused LinkedIn outreach system.

A targeted campaign run across two LinkedIn accounts over four months — built to start real conversations with the right leaders, not to blast a membership offer.

01 / Targeting

Audience Research & Targeting

We focused on US leaders most likely to value personal growth, leadership development, accountability, and sustainable performance.

  • Founders, CEOs, COOs, and managing partners
  • Senior business leaders across the US
  • Lists built from job titles and company data
  • Filtered for professional relevance
02 / Outreach

Personalized Outreach

Tailored connection messages opened conversations rather than pitching a membership on the first touch.

  • Messages written to start a conversation
  • Framed around balancing performance and well-being
  • Touched on relationships and long-term goals
  • Relevant follow-ups for interested prospects
03 / Optimization

Consistent Follow-Up

Two accounts widened coverage while messaging was refined from real engagement data every week.

  • Outreach run across two LinkedIn accounts
  • Tuned on acceptance rate and response quality
  • Focused on genuinely interested prospects
  • Moved fits to intro calls or discovery sessions
